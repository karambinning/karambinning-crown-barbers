About the shop

An old craft, kept honest.

Turkish barbering is patient work — clippers, scissors, razor, hot towel, a measured hand. We do it that way because it’s the way it’s meant to be done.

Crown Barbers sits a short walk from King’s Lynn’s old market — a small, traditional gents shop where the kettle’s on, the chair’s warm and the work speaks for itself.

We keep things uncomplicated. There’s no app to download, no booking-portal labyrinth, no upsell. You walk in, you take a seat, and you leave looking sharper than you came.

Our regulars come from across PE30 — lads on a lunch break, fathers in with sons, grooms before the wedding, gents who’ve been visiting the same chair for years. New faces are always welcome.

If you’ve never had a proper hot-towel wet shave, this is the place to start.
